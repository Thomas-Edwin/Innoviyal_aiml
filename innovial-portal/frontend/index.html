About MCET

Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology — A Premier Institution

A Legacy of Excellence

Established in 1998, Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is an autonomous institution affiliated with Anna University, Chennai. The college is accredited by NAAC with 'A++' Grade and several programs are accredited by NBA.

The Department of CSE (AI & ML) offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and industry-aligned curriculum to prepare students for careers in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Who We Are

The Department of CSE (AI & ML) — Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a premier academic department dedicated to shaping the next generation of AI professionals. Established with a vision to excel in AI and ML education, the department offers a comprehensive curriculum that blends theoretical foundations with hands-on practical experience. Our state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty, and industry-aligned programs ensure that students are well-prepared for careers in the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.

"Empowering the Future with Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning"
Building Tomorrow's AI Leaders Today

🎯

Our Vision

To become a leading department that inspires future innovators, promotes cutting-edge research, and drives meaningful advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning education and application.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide quality education in AI & ML through an industry-aligned curriculum, foster research and innovation, develop professional competencies, and nurture ethical, socially responsible AI professionals.

💡

Academic Excellence

The department offers a rigorous B.E. program in CSE (AI & ML) with courses covering machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, data science, and more. Our curriculum is regularly updated to reflect the latest industry trends and research developments.

🏭

Industry Collaboration

We maintain strong partnerships with leading organizations like IBM, TCS, Infosys, and EPAM. Through MoUs, guest lectures, internships, and placement drives, students gain invaluable industry exposure. Our dedicated placement cell ensures excellent career opportunities.

From the HOD's Desk

A message for Innoviyal — The New Student Association

Dr. Vivekanandan P
PROFESSOR & HOD (I/C)
"

It gives me immense pride and joy to announce the launch of our department's new student association — Innoviyal — set to begin its journey in August 2025. Innoviyal is more than just an association; it is a vision brought to life by the collective passion, creativity, and dedication of our students.

With a dynamic team of 39 committee members, Innoviyal aims to be the heartbeat of innovation and excellence within our department. It will serve as a vibrant platform where academic rigor meets inventive thinking — encouraging students to explore bold ideas, engage in impactful research, and participate in a variety of enriching academic and extracurricular initiatives.

I am confident that Innoviyal will grow into a thriving hub of collaboration, inspiration, and leadership. The foundation laid by this talented group of students will undoubtedly shape a legacy of achievements and elevate the reputation of our department in the years to come.

To all the founding members — your enthusiasm and commitment are the pillars upon which Innoviyal stands. I wholeheartedly extend my best wishes as you embark on this exciting journey. May this be the beginning of something truly remarkable.

Let us innovate, inspire, and lead — together.
