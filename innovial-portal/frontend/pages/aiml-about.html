Who We Are

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at MCET serves as a platform for academic excellence, technical innovation, and professional development. Through research-driven initiatives, collaborative projects, and industry engagement, we empower members to explore the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

We are a community of passionate learners, dedicated educators, and innovative researchers united by a shared vision — to harness the power of AI and ML for creating meaningful impact in society. Our department is driven by curiosity, collaboration, and a commitment to pushing the boundaries of what intelligent technology can achieve.

At the heart of our department lies a vibrant ecosystem where students are encouraged to think critically, experiment boldly, and innovate continuously. From freshmen discovering the fundamentals of programming to final-year students publishing research papers and building real-world AI applications, every member of our community contributes to a culture of excellence and lifelong learning.

Connecting students, innovators, and industry leaders to explore, build, and shape the future of intelligent technology. We are committed to fostering a culture of innovation, continuous learning, and technological excellence — preparing our graduates to become leaders, entrepreneurs, and changemakers in the rapidly evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Program Outcomes

POs — On successful completion of B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme, graduating students will be able to:

PO1
Engineering Knowledge: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.
PO2
Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
PO3
Design/Development of Solutions: Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety.
PO4
Conduct Investigations: Use research-based knowledge and methods to provide valid conclusions for complex engineering problems.
PO5
Modern Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools to complex engineering activities.
PO6
Engineer & Society: Apply reasoning to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues relevant to professional engineering practice.
PO7
Environment & Sustainability: Understand the impact of engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts and demonstrate knowledge for sustainable development.
PO8
Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities in engineering practice.
PO9
Individual & Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams and multidisciplinary settings.
PO10
Communication: Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and society at large.
PO11
Project Management: Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles for project management.
PO12
Lifelong Learning: Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and lifelong learning.
